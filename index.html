03 · Engineering approach

Guarantees should be testable, reviewable and difficult to bypass.

Position

I prefer systems where important properties are enforced: architecture rules that fail CI, database grants that block invalid writes, and tests that try to break a guarantee instead of merely confirming the happy path.

Principles
  • 01

    Make boundaries explicit.

  • 02

    Prefer evidence over claims.

  • 03

    Design recovery before celebrating throughput.

  • 04

    Keep security as an engineering property, not a costume.

  • 05

    Use the smallest public story that accurately represents the work.
